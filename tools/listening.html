%%DOWNLOADS%%

MP3 is the smaller file for everyday listening. WAV is the larger file for editing.

Save it on your phone
  • iPhone / iPad: tap Download MP3, then confirm Download in Safari. Find it in Files → Downloads. If your browser opens the audio instead, use its Share menu → Save to Files.
  • Android: tap Download MP3. Find it in Chrome → Downloads or your phone’s Files app.

After downloading, you can listen away from the room or send the file to friends. This does not add it automatically to Apple Music or Spotify.

Share in the room

Friends need the same room Wi-Fi. Keep the room PC and JamRoom Importer open while they download.

If the page will not open, check the phone is on the room Wi-Fi. Guest Wi-Fi may prevent devices from reaching the room PC. Ask the host to reopen the link if the PC’s address changed.

The recording is stored on the room PC. Download it before you leave; this local link will not work over mobile data.
